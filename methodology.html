Jurisdiction levels

Inputs:

  • FBI Crime Data Explorer agency estimates, state estimates, SRS, NIBRS and CIUS
  • State and local official publications in the reviewed source registries
  • Official police-service footprints and Census block ownership
  • Eight annual jurisdiction panels, 2018–2025

Clean annual panel row:

  • 12 reported months
  • observation weight 1
  • no filled component
  • no partial component

Temporal kernels:

OffenseKernel
MurderExponential weighting, 1-year half-life, information posterior
Rape, robbery, aggravated assaultExponential weighting, 2-year half-life
Burglary, larceny, motor vehicle theftExponential weighting, 1-year half-life

Hierarchical estimate:

  • Recipient minimum: one clean year for municipal controls
  • Peer-reference minimum: two clean years
  • Peer key: offense × state × jurisdiction class × population band
  • Shrinkage constant: 0.5
  • State nonmunicipal remainder recipient minimum: two clean years
  • Statewide overlap controls: accounting fallback
  • Locked structural zeros: retained at zero
  • Verified source replacements: retained before national calibration

Undercovered state-remainder controls:

  • Reference remainders: at least two clean years and at least 100,000 residents
  • State feature: municipal controls below 50,000 residents
  • Target: state-specific small-municipality rate × pooled remainder/small-municipality ratio
  • Current-reporting coverage blend
  • Accounting value as lower floor
  • Held-out-state common-case mean Poisson deviance: 904.5
  • All-municipality comparison: 1,212.9
  • Pooled national-remainder comparison: 1,212.9
  • Robbery comparison: 217.5, 428.9 and 335.5

National offense totals use the same temporal kernels on FBI CDE state estimates. One uniform offense-wide factor reconciles the local estimates to each national anchor. There is no state, county, jurisdiction or map-edge calibration.

Police-service footprints

  • Exclusive primary patrol ownership at Census-block support
  • One control per actual service provider or regional group
  • Pennsylvania municipal, contracted, regional and State Police service areas from the reviewed PCCD registry
  • Maryland county police and State Police reporter/service identities localized to their service areas
  • Cross-state tribal footprints retained as shared service identities where reviewed
  • Concurrent overlap agencies carried separately from primary patrol ownership
  • Unresolved overlap mass published in the unlocated-mass companion table

Neighborhood allocation

Local jurisdiction totals are distributed inside their service footprints.

  • Direct geocoded incident shares: offense-specific reviewed city feeds
  • Other areas: three-expert allocation mixture
  • Experts: empirical baseline, bounded downward GBM and opportunity exposure
  • Mixture weights: configs/mixture_ship_weights_v3.csv
  • Direct protected-class fields: excluded
  • Between-jurisdiction-only and protected proxy classes: excluded from neighborhood transfer
  • Murder: one tract estimator for direct-feed and modeled areas; 10-year incident half-life; 100-incident tract prior
  • Rape: tract-level information shrinkage
  • Murder and rape block-group expected counts: within-tract allocation for reconciliation only
  • Published block-group counts plus unlocated mass reconcile to the jurisdiction control
  • No post-model spatial smoothing
  • No cross-border feathering

Measures:

  • Activity-adjusted offense index
  • Resident offense index
  • Expected annual count
  • Crime density per square mile
  • Activity-adjusted score across seven offenses
  • Personal activity-adjusted score
  • Property activity-adjusted score
  • Resident event-burden indexes
  • Resident harm-burden index at tract support

Activity-adjusted aggregate scores are dimensionless relative scores. They are not probabilities or per-resident rates.

Special-use areas

Types:

  • Ordinary
  • Campus or institution
  • Prison or institutional
  • Park or open space
  • Transient destination
  • Industrial employment
  • Unknown special use

Publication rules:

  • Special-use type: annotation only
  • Activity-adjusted measure: offense-specific denominator and floor
  • Resident measure: resident population denominator and 50-resident floor
  • Near-zero-resident activity denominator floor: 500 when resident population is below 50
  • Missing block-group value: parent-tract value at the same estimand where available

Limitations

  • Police-recorded crime only
  • Unreported crime excluded
  • Reporting definitions and practices vary by agency and state
  • Modeled neighborhood allocation outside reviewed direct-incident feeds
  • Murder and rape not published at block-group rate support
  • Tourist and event populations not fully represented in exposure denominators
  • Los Angeles has no admitted direct-incident allocation feed in this edition
  • Sparse small-area counts produce wide uncertainty intervals
  • Special-use classification is display metadata; denominator floors still apply
  • 2020 boundaries do not represent later boundary changes
  • Alaska, Hawaii and territories excluded
  • Indexes do not estimate an individual person's probability of victimization
